# Figure 8 spec v1 (FROZEN before any slide-level cluster/GIScore statistic was computed)

Date: 2026-10-12. Template analogue: npj Precis Oncol 2026 template digital-pathology figure (tiles -> features -> PCA -> k-means (6) -> t-SNE -> representative tiles -> whole-slide maps -> classifier).
Question: is the prognostic GIScore (Fig 4; bulk RNA only) reflected in H&E morphology of the same TCGA-PAAD tumors?
NOTE (stated before running): GIScore could not be validated at single-cell / spatial resolution (Figs 5-7). A morphology association is an independent, orthogonal check; a null is publishable as a limitation.

## Data
- Features: Mahmood Lab pre-extracted UNI2-h embeddings (HF dataset MahmoodLab/UNI2-h-features; 256x256 px at 20x, ViT-H/14, 1536-d, CC-BY-NC-ND-4.0). Resolved through `UNI2H_FEATURE_ROOT` (symlinked as `01_raw/uni2h_features` in the analysis workspace); the embeddings are not redistributed.
  Deviation from template: UNI2-h replaces ImageNet ResNet-50 (documented in Methods). ResNet-50 not run.
- Cohort: the 146 TCGA-PAAD ductal patients with frozen GIScore (04_results/2026-10-12_Figure4_GALNT_run01/training_scores.csv, column `risk`). One slide per patient = DX1 (lowest DX index).
  Patients without a DX slide are excluded and listed. GIScore is the frozen Fig 4 value; not recomputed.
- Covariates: grade (G1-2 vs G3-4; H_clinical_table_TCGA_samples.csv), tissue source site (TSS = barcode characters 6-7; sites with <5 patients pooled as "other"). Stage is NOT used (139/146 Stage I-II).
- CPTAC-PDA (557 slides, mean ~2.7k tiles/slide vs ~10k for TCGA, tiling differs): processed separately, descriptive only; not pooled with TCGA. No GIScore claim unless GALNT14/16 RNA is verified for the cohort (not done in this spec).

## Frozen pipeline
1. Tile sampling: up to 2,000 tiles per slide, random without replacement, seed 20261012 (slides with fewer tiles: all). No tissue re-filtering beyond Mahmood Lab's own tile list.
2. Standardise features (global mean/SD across sampled tiles) -> PCA to 32 components (template). Sensitivity: 64 components.
3. k-means, k = 6 (template), n_init = 10, seed 20261012. Sensitivity (supplement): silhouette on a 50k-tile subsample for k = 3-10; k = 5, 7 label concordance (ARI). k = 6 is NOT chosen by silhouette.
4. t-SNE (2D) on a stratified 30k-tile subsample (perplexity 30, seed 20261012), PCA-32 input.
5. Per-slide cluster fractions (6 values). Cluster labels are descriptive only: morphological names are assigned from representative tiles (the 12 tiles nearest the cluster centroid, from 12 different slides) and are NOT pathologist-verified. Labels are written only after the statistics are computed.
6. Slide-image panels use GDC DX1 SVS for a small set of display slides only; features and statistics never depend on SVS files.

## Hypotheses and tests (all two-sided; BH across the 6 clusters)
- H8a (primary): cluster fraction (CLR-transformed with pseudocount 1e-3) is associated with continuous GIScore: OLS fraction ~ GIScore + grade + TSS (fixed effects, pooled sites), standardised beta for GIScore with HC3 SE. Unadjusted Spearman reported alongside. Success: >= 1 cluster with BH < 0.05 in the adjusted model, same sign as unadjusted.
- H8b: patient-level MIL classifier predicts GIScore High vs Low (frozen split at training median: 73/73). Attention-based MIL on frozen UNI2-h features (single attention head, 256 hidden, dropout 0.25, Adam, lr 2e-4, 30 epochs, batch = 1 slide, tiles sampled as in step 1 each epoch).
  Patient-stratified 5-fold CV repeated 5 times (different fold seeds); report mean and SD of fold AUC and pooled out-of-fold AUC. Permutation null: 200 label shuffles of the whole 5-fold procedure (single repeat). Success: pooled AUC > 0.5 with permutation p < 0.05.
  Baseline: logistic regression (L2) on mean-pooled UNI2-h features per slide, same CV.
- H8c (confound control): same CV classifier predicting (i) TSS (top-3 sites vs rest, one-vs-rest macro AUC) and (ii) grade. If TSS is predicted at least as well as GIScore, GIScore AUC is labelled site-confounded. Also repeat H8b within a TSS-stratified setting (CV folds balanced by TSS; and a leave-site-out sensitivity on the 3 largest sites).
- Heat maps: attention weights from the MIL model are used instead of Grad-CAM (features are precomputed; no pixel-level gradient exists). Shown only for slides correctly classified in the out-of-fold prediction. Deviation from template stated in the caption.
- Null results are reported. No re-clustering, no k tuning, no removal of slides after viewing results.

## Results addendum (computed 2026-10-12)
- 145 of 146 patients have a DX slide (TCGA-H6-8124 excluded); 269,259 tiles; PCA-32 explains 40.8% of variance. Silhouette k = 3-10 is low and flat (0.12-0.15), i.e. continuous morphology space; k = 6 kept as frozen.
- Sensitivity: ARI(k6 PCA32 vs PCA64) = 0.71, ARI(k6 vs k5) = 0.92, ARI(k6 vs k7) = 0.76.
- H8a: C1 (6.9% of tiles) fraction decreases with GIScore: adjusted beta = -0.34 SD (95% CI -0.57, -0.12), BH = 0.017 (unadjusted BH 0.0003, Spearman rho = -0.27). No other cluster passes. C2, C3, C4, C6 are mostly tissue-source-site/stain clusters (R2 of TSS alone 0.57-0.89); C1 is far less site-driven (R2 0.23).
- H8b: MIL 5-fold x 5 repeats: fold AUC 0.62 +/- 0.09, pooled AUC 0.62 (range 0.58-0.68 across repeats); mean-pooled logistic baseline pooled AUC 0.62 (permutation p = 0.035 for the baseline, 200 shuffles).
- H8c: the same MIL predicts TSS (4-class macro AUC) at 0.97 versus 0.62 for GIScore, so site is the dominant signal in these embeddings. GIScore AUC with TSS-stratified folds 0.65 (fold) / 0.62 (pooled); leave-one-site-out AUC: IB 0.62 (n = 38), HZ 0.80 (n = 22), FB 0.52 (n = 16). Grade AUC 0.63.

### A10 addendum 1 (Q1 result and Q4 ink QC; written before ink detection is run on the cohort; 2026-10-12)
Q1 result (pilot, 15 slides): re-read WSI thumbnails and scanner-embedded thumbnails agree in colour (no decode/RGB error); slide-level colour of the 7 flagged slides is normal pink-purple. The two blue C5 representative tiles (TCGA-3A-A9J0 tile 3074, TCGA-F2-A44G tile 10342) lie on hand-drawn marker ink on the glass slide (dark-blue ring; dark-green strokes). Hence the C5 blue appearance is local ink contamination, not H&E staining batch. Stain normalisation (Macenko/Reinhard) cannot remove ink.
Q4 (POST HOC QC sensitivity analysis added after Q1; it does NOT replace the frozen primary Fig 8 analysis, which is retained as published in this project): ink filtering.
1. Detection uses only images/coordinates/embeddings; GIScore, grade and group labels are NOT loaded in the detection code. Rule: slide thumbnail (max side ~2400 px) -> Lab; ink pixels = non-tissue-like chroma (blue: a* < 8 and b* < -22; green/teal: a* < -6; black marker: L* < 22 and chroma < 14) after removal of specks; require stroke-like/connected structure (connected components >= 30 thumbnail pixels at ds ~ 40-64) and tile ink fraction >= 10% (core) or touching a core tile (edge, 1-tile dilation). Thresholds were checked on the pilot slides only, not tuned on GIScore.
2. Manual QC: overlay sheets of all slides with any ink-flagged tile and of the highest-C5 slides are inspected; false-positive rules (dark haematoxylin nuclei, folds) are corrected by changing the rule globally, never per slide, and the change is logged here.
3. Recorded: fraction of ink-flagged tiles per slide, site, frozen cluster of flagged tiles.
4. Re-computation uses the already cached UNI2-h embeddings and the FROZEN standardise+PCA+k-means (no re-clustering; cluster numbering unchanged): the same sampled tiles minus ink tiles -> C1-C6 fractions -> H8a model (CLR, grade + site covariates, HC3, BH over 6). Report before/after: C1 beta (direction, size, CI), C5 fraction, per-patient changes, and the site-classification AUC of Fig 8I (mean-pooled features without ink tiles).
5. Slides with a high ink-tile fraction (including TCGA-F2-A44G) are inspected separately: tissue morphology vs stain density vs ink.
6. Fig 8 is not edited (C5 label and examples unchanged) until Q4 is reviewed. If C5 is ink-enriched it will be described as an artifact-enriched cluster.
7. No cohort-wide Macenko re-extraction is started.

### A10 addendum 2 (Q4 results; POST HOC QC sensitivity analysis; primary analysis unchanged; 2026-10-12)
Method as in addendum 1 (rule v2 in 03_analysis/57_figure8_ink_rule.py: navy/dark-blue, green/teal, black marker; scan-border strips and border bars removed; core tile = ink >= 10% of tile footprint at ds 32-64; edge = 8-neighbour of a core tile). Rule v1 -> v2 change (made after viewing the 15 pilot thumbnails only, before any cohort result or outcome data): added navy rule (v1 missed the dark-blue ring of TCGA-3A-A9J0), hue-based green rule, border-strip removal. Detection code never loaded GIScore/grade/group (58_figure8_ink_tiles.py reads only patient/file/site). Thumbnails: all 145 slides (02_intermediate/fig8_ink).
Manual QC of overlays (blind to GIScore): true ink found in TCGA-3A-A9J0 (dark-blue ring), TCGA-F2-A44G (green strokes), TCGA-FB-AAPP (black/blue marker blob; additionally a large RED marker ring around the tissue that the rule does not detect), TCGA-PZ-A5RE (blue/green squiggle, non-tissue object that received tiles). False positives noted: thin lines along tissue edges (TCGA-HZ-7924, HZ-7919, HZ-A4BH), dense dark-blue lymphoid tissue (TCGA-IB-AAUP), scanner corner/border strips (TCGA-F2-A7TX). No per-slide manual edits were applied; the automatic flags were used as they are (false positives only make the filter more conservative). Limitations: red ink not detected; thumbnail resolution ds 32-64 (tile = 8-16 px) misses small marks.
Results (145 slides, 269,259 sampled tiles): ink core tiles 0.15%, core+edge 0.41% (1,112 tiles); 47 slides have >= 1 core tile, 15 slides > 1% (core+edge), 4 slides > 5%. Flagged tiles by frozen cluster: C2 44%, C4 26%, C1 9%, C3 9%, C6 7%, C5 4.5%; fraction of each cluster that is ink: C5 0.36% (C1 0.58%, C2 0.60%).
Before/after (same sampled tiles minus core+edge, frozen clusters, H8a model): C1 beta -0.342 (95% CI -0.567 to -0.117; BH 0.0173) -> -0.345 (-0.569 to -0.121; BH 0.0151); Spearman rho C1 -0.270 -> -0.270; C5 mean fraction 0.0500 -> 0.0500 (beta -0.002 -> -0.005, BH 0.99). Other clusters change in the 3rd decimal. ABMIL on ink-free bags: TSS 4-class macro AUC 0.9705 -> 0.9715; GIScore ABMIL pooled AUC 0.624 -> 0.629 (fold mean 0.623 -> 0.633); mean-pool baseline unchanged (0.619); grade 0.625 -> 0.639; site-stratified folds 0.646 -> 0.640.
Interpretation: ink is real but rare; it does NOT explain C5 (C5 is not ink-enriched) nor the C1-GIScore association nor the site-classification AUC of 0.97. The two blue C5 representative tiles were ink tiles; they were selected as nearest-to-centroid tiles from distinct slides and are not typical of C5. Thumbnail-derived tile colour: C5 tiles are darker and bluer (median L* 51.7, b* -14.3) than other clusters (L* 62-70, b* -6 to -10). Non-ink C5 tiles of TCGA-F2-A44G/2J-AABU/US-A77G (C5_nonink_tiles_*.png) are densely cellular epithelium with deep haematoxylin staining. TCGA-F2-A44G: C5 53% of sampled tiles, ink core 0.9%; the slide is deeply stained (median tile L* 54.7 vs cohort 65.8). So C5 = real dense, strongly stained cellular tissue; staining intensity (slide-dependent) contributes; the "stain artefact" label is only partly right and "artifact-enriched" is NOT supported.
Fig 8 is not edited. Phase 1 pilot (Q2/Q3: feature reproduction, Macenko/Reinhard) is NOT finished: the remote tile reads are too slow (about 20 s/tile); tile reading will be redone with fewer tiles/slide before Q2/Q3, if still wanted.
